This site is built so that your work - your choices, your text, the sheet itself - stays with you, on your device, and is never sent to a server.

## The prayer sheet is built in your browser

Building the prayer sheet, including assembling it into a PDF, happens entirely in your browser - using a layout engine compiled to WebAssembly that runs locally on your device. The sheet's content (names, choices, text) is never sent to our server, and we do not keep a copy of it.

## What is stored on your device

A few small preferences are saved to the browser's localStorage so you do not have to set them again on every visit: interface language, theme (light/dark), and the accessibility preferences you chose in the accessibility widget. This information stays on your device only and is not accessible to us.

## Offline caching (PWA)

The site caches a copy of its interface files (code, styling, fonts) on your device via a Service Worker, so pages load quickly and remain available without an internet connection. This is a technical browser mechanism and does not collect information about you.

## Contact form

If you choose to fill out the contact form, the email address and message you enter are sent to a third-party service called FormSubmit, which forwards the message to us by email. We do not use this information for anything other than replying to your message.

## What we do not do

The site has no tracking code, no Google Analytics, and no advertising cookies. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information with any third party other than the transfer required to deliver the contact form, as described above.

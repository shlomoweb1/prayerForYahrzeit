**Prompt**

> **Ultra-photorealistic cinematic memorial portrait, 8K quality, premium photography, centered composition, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, golden hour lighting.**
>
> A modern Jewish woman in her early 30s to early 40s stands perfectly centered in a peaceful Jewish cemetery at sunset. She has natural Mediterranean Jewish features, warm olive skin, dark brown shoulder-length hair partially covered with a simple beige headscarf (not overly religious), a calm and respectful expression, and her eyes are gently lowered in reflection.
>
> She wears elegant modest modern clothing: an off-white linen blouse with sleeves rolled slightly, a dark flowing skirt, and minimal gold jewelry. Her appearance should feel timeless and universally Jewish, suitable for Ashkenazi, Sephardi, and Mizrahi communities, without looking specifically Haredi or belonging to any single denomination.
>
> She gently holds a traditional Jewish memorial candle (Ner Neshama) with both hands at chest height. The candle emits a soft warm glow that subtly illuminates her hands and face.
>
> She stands among rows of authentic Jewish gravestones made of Jerusalem stone, each with naturally engraved Hebrew inscriptions and subtle Magen David symbols. The cemetery contains olive trees, cypress trees, and native Israeli vegetation. The distant background shows softly blurred rolling Jerusalem hills and warm golden sky, **without any mosques, churches, Temple Mount, Dome of the Rock, modern skyline, or recognizable landmarks.**
>
> The atmosphere is peaceful, hopeful, and full of remembrance rather than sadness. Soft haze, volumetric sunlight, cinematic color grading, gentle lens bloom, realistic skin texture, natural clothing folds, highly detailed stone textures, and realistic candlelight reflections.
>
> Symmetrical composition with the woman centered in frame. Plenty of negative space around the subject so it works as a website hero image. Warm cream, beige, gold, olive green, and soft stone colors. Professional editorial photography, museum-quality realism, HDR, global illumination, physically accurate lighting, ultra-detailed, natural facial anatomy, emotionally authentic.
